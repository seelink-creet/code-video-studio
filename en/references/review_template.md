# Self-review template
Be honest, give timestamps, cite evidence (contact sheet, strips, crops, qc_motion plot, loudness report); write "cannot verify" where true.
```
## "Title" v{N} self-review
Specs: {W}×{H} {fps} fps {dur} s | HD {MB} / chat {MB} | {LUFS} / {dBTP} (measured after AAC)
### What works (max 3)
1. {time}: {what} — {why; which principle: hook / speed ramp / text in the world / bookend …}
### What doesn't (at least 2, most serious first)
1. {time}: {problem} — {evidence} — {proposed fix + effort}
### Against the brief
length/aspect/text/URL/must-avoid: {met?}; each key beat of the beat sheet: {present?}
### What I cannot verify
listening quality (I checked levels, onsets, pitch); phone speaker; platform recompression
### Next step (user chooses)
A deliver | B fix items {x,y} | C new ending/direction, two versions to compare
```
Self-check questions: motion and sound on frame 0? any uniform or still stretch >2.5 s? does text look typed on the screen or grown in the world? does the ending carry the climax and bookend? is the hero big enough on arrival? cheapness sources: copy-paste crowds, GM sounds, one looping chord progression, generic dissolves.
