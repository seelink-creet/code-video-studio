# Proven technical lessons
**Rendering:** frames depend only on t; draw random values once at init with a fixed seed; no system time; no state carried between frames (cache sims per frame or every N frames) · chunked parallel render + concat; an interrupted encode leaves an mp4 without moov → re-concat the finished chunks · judge energy effects at full resolution (half-res scales gas/line brightness differently) · keep one blocking command alive on long jobs; watch memory · PIL `GaussianBlur` needs a Python `float` radius · re-view images under new file names (tool caches).
**Speed & camera:** keys (τ, speed) with smoothstep in output time; segment length = Δτ/mean(s0,s1); render frame(τ(T)) · streak length `speed·(1+0.55·max(speed−1,0))/48` s of story time; drive twinkles by a mix of output and story time or they die in freezes · plot speed, audio level and frame diff on one axis — it catches a held swell killing a slow-mo dip and a layer cut off hard · smooth the camera heading strongly (σ≈8 frames) with ~0.4 s look-ahead · in near-static holds don't quantise per-frame lookup tables by camera distance (periodic whole-frame jumps); compute per frame and detect jumps relative to neighbouring frames · pull-back reveals: exponential in log distance (fast → slow) with the look point shifted by (d−d0)/(D−d0) · huge depth ranges: brightness ∝ (REF/z)² with a cap; depth jitter in world units.
**Text:** text that "looks typed on the screen" is fixed only by putting glyphs in the world (rasterise into world/polar textures and sample them through the same projection) · lensed or mirrored text: test with a debug phrase (e.g. "FRLPG") and check glyph by glyph — order can be right while each glyph is upside down (rotate the strip 180°) · wrap borders when remapping polar textures · CJK "dust into characters": sample targets inside stroke outlines, arrival time from the nearest centre-line parameter → particles land in stroke order; Latin handwriting: hand-placed Catmull-Rom keys, speed by curvature, thick downstrokes · cache each (glyph, size) raster once; check the font covers every character.
**Physics & particles:** text into objects: Poisson samples in the mask, ~22% snapped to the outline, `linear_sum_assignment` with random cost, quadratic Bézier + sideways swirl · deformable parts stored in (s,t) parameter space · bursts without white-out: global lift ≤0.07, gas layer fades in over ~1.4 s, 4 sub-samples of radial motion blur.
**Splicing & delivery:** change endings by splicing (old silent master up to the cut + new ending), one final encode, check frame diff at the join · re-encode to "target MB × 8 ÷ duration" when over a 100 MB limit (`deliver.py`) · non-integer scaling blurs pixel art; enlarge small symbols on key beats · captions/CTA as a separate transparent layer.
